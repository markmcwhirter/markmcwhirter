About

Mark McWhirter has dedicated his life to ministry, leadership, and global outreach, serving in various capacities across the United States and abroad. Raised in Fairbanks and a 35-year resident of Alaska, he has made a lasting impact on the community through his pastoral work, mentorship, and devotion to faith. With a passion for ministry that has spanned decades, Mark has pastored churches in Alaska, California, and Arizona, bringing spiritual guidance and encouragement to diverse congregations. His commitment to spreading the gospel also led him to serve as a missionary in France for two years, where he ministered to local communities and engaged in evangelistic efforts. Beyond his pastoral and missionary work, Mark has traveled extensively, sharing his faith and ministering in the states, Europe and Australia. His international experiences have strengthened his perspective on ministry, fostering a deep appreciation for cross-cultural outreach and global discipleship. Mark received bachelors and masters degrees in computer science, has a bachelor’s degree in pastoral leadership, a masters in divinity, and has a Doctor of Theology (Th.D.) from Life Point Christian University in Glendale, Arizona. His academic and theological training have enriched his preaching, teaching, and leadership, equipping him to serve with wisdom and biblical insight. Mark resides in Phoenix, Arizona, has three daughters: Anna, Emma, and Victoria and four grandchildren.
